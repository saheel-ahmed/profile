Lawha لوحة

Visual work management, built bilingual.

A briefing for business owners · September 2026

The problem

Your work already exists. It just isn't anywhere.

  • Decisions live in chatSearchable by whoever remembers the right word
  • Status lives in someone's headWhich is why you hold the meeting
  • The tracker is always behindBecause updating it is someone's least favourite job
  • Nobody is wrongEveryone is working from a slightly different picture
The cost

What that actually costs you

Twice
the same work started in two places
Weekly
meetings held only to establish status
Days
lost per new joiner learning where things live
Late
deadlines discovered rather than predicted

None of this shows up as a line item. It shows up as slowness.

The idea

One board. Everyone sees the same thing.

Work becomes a card. Cards sit in lists. Lists sit on a board. Anyone can read it on sight — no training, no manual, no explaining what the columns mean.

Adoption

It earns its place in the first week

The reason simple tools win is that people actually use them. Lawha is designed so the first board takes five minutes and the habit forms before anyone has to be convinced.

Automation

The board does the administration

Every recurring chore — chasing, copying, updating, summarising — becomes a rule you set once. This is where the hours come back.

Intelligence

It sets things up so people don't have to

  • Describe a goal, get a boardStructured lists and cards, in Arabic or English
  • Checklists write themselvesFrom what the card already says
  • Duplicates merge cleanlyNothing is deleted, everything is reversible
  • Focus time is found for youIt reads the calendar, it never books without asking

Every AI action is reviewable and undoable. That is the point.

Differentiator

Arabic and English, equally

Most tools bolt Arabic on late — the layout breaks, search stops working, the AI gets worse. Lawha is built right-to-left from the first line, so an Arabic-speaking team gets the same product, not a compromised one.

For leadership

You stop asking for status

The same cards your teams work in become the timeline and the dashboard you read. Nobody prepares it. Nobody formats it. It is simply what is true right now.

Control

Governance that doesn't slow anyone down

Policy is set once at the top and inherits downward. Single sign-on, provisioning, audit trails and UAE data residency are platform properties — not something a team has to remember to switch on.

Fit

It joins your stack rather than replacing it

Nothing is trapped. Native connectors cover the tools you already run, an open API covers everything else, and AI assistants can read and write boards directly.

Measurement

How we'll know it worked

  • Time to first cardAdoption friction, measured in minutes
  • Share of work created automaticallyHow much admin the system absorbed
  • Status meetings removedThe most honest measure of visibility
  • Proportion of sessions in ArabicWhether the bilingual promise held

Agree the baseline before the pilot, not after.
